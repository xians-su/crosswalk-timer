# crosswalk-timer

Countdown for crossing 南京西路 at 赤峰街 (Taipei): is it my turn, how many seconds are left, and when to leave.

- `index.html` – the page (also contains a Scriptable widget). It computes everything from the device clock and `schedule.json`.
- `schedule.json` – timetable generated by `build.py` from the official timing plan (`plans.json`, parsed by `parse_plan.py` from 臺北市交通管制工程處's public PDF) plus camera corrections (`calib.json`).
- `logger.py` + `check.py` – sample the public traffic camera and compare the lamp with the timetable. Results accumulate in `checks.json`.
- `.github/workflows/` – weekly refresh of the official plan; camera checks in every timing band on one weekday and one weekend day.

Timing data: 臺北市政府交通局 open data (臺北市路口號誌時制計畫).
